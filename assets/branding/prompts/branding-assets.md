# Branding image prompts

These are the original prompts used with the built-in ImageGen tool. All generated art was moved into this project; the original generated files remain in the Codex generated-images library.

## Main background

Use case: scientific-educational. Create a reusable full-frame 9:16 vertical background for 1080×1920 bilingual English-learning Reels: a sophisticated bright warm-ivory paper surface, matte finish, very fine paper grain and barely visible monochrome noise, softly brighter center, extremely subtle cream edge falloff, no horizon or objects. Keep the center and upper/lower regions uncluttered for dark text cards. Palette cream #F6F0E5 with warm ivory. No text, letters, logo, character, speech bubbles, cards, border, watermark, stains, or focal objects.

## Warm background

Use case: scientific-educational. Create a reusable portrait 9:16 warm variation for a 1080×1920 bilingual learning video: seamless matte warm-ivory paper with fine even grain, central two thirds evenly lit and quiet for dark text cards, faint warm edge shading, gentle late-afternoon cream warmth. Palette #F6F0E5 with subtly warmer beige and a tiny terracotta warmth near distant edges. No text, letters, logo, character, speech bubbles, cards, border, watermark, objects, or stains.

## Cool background

Palette #F6F0E5 with barely noticeable pale blue-gray and teal cooling near the distant edges. No text, letters, logo, character, speech bubbles, cards, border, watermark, objects, or stains.

## Female teacher

Use case: illustration-story. Create an original transparent cutout for the lower-right of a Vietnamese English-learning Reel. One friendly young Vietnamese woman teacher with shoulder-length slightly wavy dark-brown hair, no glasses, warm expressive smile, teal cardigan over a cream shirt, holding a small cream study notebook with a blank teal bookmark; her other hand gently gestures toward the center/left. Polished 2D editorial illustration, clean soft outlines, flat layered color, subtle paper-like shading, modern educational magazine art, not 3D. Three-quarter upper body down to mid-thigh, face and both hands fully visible, centered in portrait framing with transparent margins. Palette teal, cream, dark brown and restrained terracotta. Actual transparent alpha. No scenery, extra characters, furniture, logo, text, watermark, bun hairstyle, or orange top.
